Our church plan · Christ's method, step by step

→→→→

Pray together, every day of the week.

Let us become a church that prays for one another, our families, our church, and our community. Our goal is a prayer group every day of the week.

Why this step? ▾
  • United prayer brings the church together and invites the Holy Spirit to lead. Before we go out, we ask God for His compassion for our neighbors.
  • A group for every day of the week means someone is praying for the church and the town every day, and every member can find a time that fits.
  • Train a second leader for each group, in case of travel or illness, so no day goes without prayer.
  • Keep a simple prayer record and share answers to prayer. It grows our faith and keeps us praying.

Go and listen.

Go to community events to get to know people, not to sell anything. Use “Find a way to serve” to pick events, then write down what you notice on your Community Needs sheet.

Why this step? ▾
  • Jesus showed us how to reach people: He mingled with them, showed sympathy, met their needs and won their trust before He said, “Follow Me.”
  • We go to community events to get to know people, not to sell anything. We learn names and listen.
  • Listen where neighbors already gather: the farmers market, the library, the rec center, community events.
  • Write down what you notice on a card for each event: who you met, the needs you heard, and who is already helping.

Learn your community.

Basic numbers for your town fill in automatically. Then add what your members heard and saw. Check the numbers with your neighbors before you act.

Why this step? ▾
  • Basic numbers help us know where to listen first, but they are only a starting point. Check them with your neighbors before you act.
  • Map who is already serving. Often the best help is joining what others already do well.
  • Start with only one or two small, steady things. Small and steady is better than big and short.
  • Be honest about our own limits. Many members live outside town and work long hours, so commitments should fit real life.

Choose needs and form teams.

Mark the ideas you feel called to do with a ✓ or a ★. Add up the marks and circle the top ideas, about one team for every five people. Then each team fills out a Team card.

Why this step? ▾
  • Everyone marks the ideas they feel called to do. We add up the marks and choose about one team for every five people.
  • Teams of two to five people, each with a leader, make sure every member has a way to serve.
  • Each team fills out a Team card: the need, the leader, the members, who to call this week, training, and the first dates.
  • Elders each care for two or three teams, and church offices help where their duties fit, so no team serves alone.

Team cards

Invite them in, then follow up.

You went to them first. Now open your doors: post your team's event so neighbors nearby can find it, follow up with everyone you met within a week, and share stories each month.

Why this step? ▾
  • We went to them first. Now we open our doors, and neighbors come to know us, like a monthly pancake breakfast.
  • Every person we meet hears from us within a week. A friendly follow-up turns a meeting into a friendship.
  • Walk with people, not just meet their needs. Pair new friends with a mentor and give them a way to serve too.
  • Share stories each month. Team leaders meet, encourage each other and thank God for what He is doing.

1. Post your team's event

Put it on the Serve map so neighbors nearby can find it. Every post is checked before it appears.

2. Follow up within a week

Everyone you met at events (from step 2). Check them off as you follow up and pair new friends with a mentor.

3. Make an invitation

Turn a team card into a simple invitation to share or print.

4. Connection card

A small card guests can keep. It sends them to your coordinator for prayer, Bible study or help. Nothing is stored online.

Print and download will work on the live site; the square is where a QR code would go.

Leading a planning session

Eight steps for one evening or Sabbath afternoon, from the Pipestone planning guide. Tap a step to open that part of your plan.

  1. Pray in small groupsGroups of three or four: thanks, forgiveness, compassion for neighbors, and the Spirit's leading.
  2. See where we areShow what the church has already done.
  3. Share what we learnedWhat needs did you see? Who did you meet? Who is already serving?
  4. Choose the needsEveryone marks ideas with ✓ or ★; circle about one idea for every five people.
  5. Form teamsA sign for each idea; people walk to where God leads, two to five per team, each with a leader.
  6. Fill in the team cardNeed, leader, members, who to call this week, training, first dates.
  7. Plan one or two group outingsPick community events to attend together in the next month.
  8. Close in prayerCommit to follow Christ's method together.
